Q1. [Easy] [Na+] What colour does a sodium salt give in the flame test?
   A) Brick red
   B) Golden yellow
   C) Lilac (pale violet)
   D) Apple green
   Answer: B) Golden yellow

Q2. [Easy] [Na+] Before a flame test, the platinum or nichrome wire is cleaned by dipping it in:
